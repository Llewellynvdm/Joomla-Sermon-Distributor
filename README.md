# Sermon Distributor (6.0.0)

 ![Sermon Distributor image](https://git.vdm.dev/christian/Joomla-Sermon-Distributor/raw/branch/master/admin/assets/images/vdm-component.jpg "The Sermon Distributor")

Sermon Distributor is a highly advanced sermon distributor that runs on the [Joomla 6x](http://www.joomla.org) framework. It is designed to help churches, ministries, and congregations manage, organize, publish, and distribute sermons with ease.

The component provides a complete sermon management system, allowing administrators to maintain sermon records together with their related preacher, church, congregation, date, time, series, and media details. Sermons can be grouped into series, linked to specific preachers, associated with churches, and published in a structured way that makes them easy to browse, search, and share.

Sermon Distributor supports both local sermon uploads and external sermon integrations. Administrators may upload sermon files directly into the component, or connect the component to an external folder where sermon files are discovered, listed, managed, and made available for download. This makes it suitable for ministries that already maintain sermon archives outside Joomla, as well as those that want to manage everything directly from within the site.

With its flexible structure, Sermon Distributor helps preserve sermon archives, simplify media distribution, and provide visitors with organized access to sermons by preacher, series, church, date, or download source. It is built to serve both small congregations and larger ministries that require an advanced, reliable, and extensible sermon distribution solution.

The best way to see all your options is to install this component on you Joomla website and test all features yourself. Here is a [direct download link](https://git.vdm.dev/christian/Joomla-Sermon-Distributor/archive/master.zip)
